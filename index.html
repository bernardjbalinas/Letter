Dear Someone Special,

Birthday ko na sa 9, diba usually kapag birthday may mga cake yan, pagbati tapos mga gifts. Di ako excited don, excited ako sa bagay na una kong gagawin ay makapagbigay ng sulat sayo. I realized it would be one of the most important things I could do this year, before I end my days as a 27 year old and start my days as a 28 year old. For me hindi naman siya dahil okasyon kaya ko sinulat, ofcourse kasama yun pero mas nangingibabaw yung rason na you're worth writing to.

Tuwing birthday, siguro birthday mo din, mas madalas nakakatanggap tayo diba. Mine is different, I think of a person I can give something to. Dati, sa labas ako nakapagbigay, last year ata sa tagabukas ng pinto sa 7/11 yung nalibre ko ng pagkain haha. Yun parang nafulfill ko sya dahil don. Anyway, I am carrying onto that tradition. Only this time, I want to give you words that come straight from me, some words that Ive been holding back, some words I am hoping will mean something to you.

I think back to how we met. Threads lang yun. I dont know, cute na yata ako agad paningin mo at agad mo hinihingi yung messenger ko (or FB ata) Hindi naman sya engrande diba na pagkakakilala, no fireworks, dramatic entrance. Basta yun, nagkausap, nagkakilala. In those quiet moments, it changed everything without us realizing it. Simple lang sya kung tutuusin, pero nagmamarka parin sakin.

Simula non, masaya naman tayo diba? Haha hanggang ngayon di mo parin ako pinapautang ng 500, ano ba yan. Takot ka bang takbuhan kita? Hahaha Ibabalik ko naman kasi sa katapusan. Parang lahat ata ng memes na sinesend ko sayo ganon noh? And when I hear you laugh, it makes me happy. I dont know why. Basta masaya lang ako pag napapasaya kita and naririnig ko tawa mo haha. Feels ordinary, parang tropa siguro, but to me, it proves how comfortable we became knowing each other.

“I do not need this letter to change anything. I only wanted it to tell the truth.”

You know, I find myself smiling when I think of those moments. Its funny na kahit ganong kaliit lang na joke or parang trippings lang, nagsestay sya. And it definitely brightens my day, knowing I had you laughing. Madalas ako down sa totoo lang, pero malaman ko lang na masaya ka that day, it definitely brightens my day pa din.

What I admire most about you is how you carry yourself. Alam mo yun, problemado ka na pero ngumingiti ngiti ka pa? hahaha hanep na yan. Although sometimes natatarayan mo ako, ewan ko ba ang bait bait ko naman hmp. Tapos you love music pa just as much as I do. tapos mahilig ka rin pala sa libro, like what the helly, dami kong nalalaman sayong bago. You show me that youre someone who feels deeply and thinks beyond the surface. Para kang paborito kong kanta na kahit kailan di naluluma, or parang page sa isang book na nandyan lang kahit nabasa mo na sya, parang gusto mo balik balikan.

You bring rhythm and meaning into even the simplest conversations. Although most of the time lately, parang lagi tayong mag asawang may bangayan, talking to you feels like listening to a melody that lingers, or opening a chapter in a story I definitely would not want to end. And honest confession right here, I catch myself thinking about you even when we are not talking. Para kang naniningil ng utang, di ka mawala sa isip ko minsan, eme. Youve become a part of my days in ways I didnt expect, sobrang grateful ako don. Youve given me reasons to smile, pag isipan mga desisyon ko sa buhay at umasa na balang araw magiging okay din ang lahat.

As I step into another year of my life, I realize that my wish is not for material things, not parties nor presents, but for more of these moments with you. More laughter, more stories, more memories waiting to be made. Youve also given me something to look forward to, hindi lang yung bukas. Something more. Thats the reason why this letter is so important to me. Hindi lang kasi birthday ko na, its about you, its about us.

And here is the truth Ive been holding quietly, the reason this letter feels so important.

Gusto kita.

Not in a casual way we sometimes say it, but in that way that makes me look forward to your presence. the way that makes me smile at the thought of you, the way that makes me hope for more. I like you in the way that makes me want to share not just jokes and stories, but pieces of myself that I dont always show, those things na di ko laging sinasabi.

I want to know more about you. I want to discover the songs that keep you moving, the stories that inspire you, the little details that make you who you are. Kasi when you share a piece of yourself, it feels like I am being invited into a world I want to know better.

Writing this is both exciting and somehow nakakakaba. Ewan ko ba. But id rather you know na than to keep it hidden for longer. So yeah let this be both a birthday gift, atleast for me to cherish, and a confession, for you to think about. A gift of words that I hope reach you, and you’ll keep close. And sa 9, hindi naman ako nagcecelebrate kasi talaga, pero simple lang ang wish ko. I hope this letter will be the first page of a bigger story, one that we might write together.

Always rooting for you, Your Name
