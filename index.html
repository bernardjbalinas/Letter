Dear Eya,

I thought about simply sending you a message, but somehow that felt too ordinary for what I wanted to say.

So this is me choosing to slow down for a moment. No noise, no expectations, no need for a perfect response. Just a page, a few honest words, and the hope that you can read them exactly as they are meant.

You have a way of making ordinary moments feel a little more memorable. A conversation becomes something I replay later. A small detail becomes something I remember. Even silence can carry more meaning than I expect.

“I do not need this letter to change anything. I only wanted it to tell the truth.”

And the truth is simple: I am grateful that our paths crossed. Whatever name belongs to this chapter of our lives, I am glad it exists.

I hope life is gentle with you. I hope you find reasons to laugh on difficult days, people who make you feel safe, and moments that remind you how much good still exists around you.

And if, every now and then, this little page reminds you that somewhere out there is a person quietly wishing you well, then I think that is enough.

Always rooting for you, Your Name
